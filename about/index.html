DEBYKO is a market-data platform for crypto derivatives. It is designed, built and run by Denisas Bykovas, and operated under licence by MB „Blynai“ — a small partnership (mažoji bendrija) registered in Vilnius, Lithuania, inside the European Union.

There is no team page, because there is no team behind it. Every collector, every stored row, every endpoint, every screen and every sentence on this site has one author. The rules on the methodology page are his rules, and the name on the product is his: DEnisas BYKOvas.

01 · The founder

He built the systems Lithuania files its taxes and moves its money through. Then he built this one.

Twenty-five years on .NET and systems architecture. Lead architect of EDS4, the national tax declaration platform of the State Tax Inspectorate. Architect and integration lead of CENTROlink, the Bank of Lithuania's gateway to the European payment rails. Architect of a shop-floor execution system for a blinds factory, and of unmanned drinking-water stations across Vilnius.

The work he takes is the work nobody else wants to own: legacy stitched to regulation stitched to a release calendar that cannot slip — systems that make the morning news if they stop. DEBYKO is what the same discipline looks like when it is pointed at crypto market data.

02 · Same rules, new market

Every rule DEBYKO follows was learned on a system that was not allowed to fail.

A national tax platform and a central bank gateway do not get to guess, round, or fill a hole with a plausible number. Neither does this one.

Where it was learnedWhat it demandedThe rule in DEBYKO
CENTROlinkEvery transaction observable by the regulatorEvery value is stored with its source and the time it was received.
EDS4Releases the regulator can audit on the wireThe methodology changes only by a dated entry, so a figure is read against the rules in force when it was shown.
CENTROlinkDeployed beside the legacy processors — no flag dayCollectors roll out venue by venue. Stored history is untouched.
LKvitai.MESOperators act on what the screen saysAn absent value is an em dash with its reason — never a zero someone might act on.
Vilniaus VandenysControl stays next to the hardwareAgent runs on the operator's machine. Keys never leave their side.
