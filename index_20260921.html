Principles

Nothing is shown as more than it is.

Six rules the product is held to. The page above is held to the same rules.

  1. 01Every value keeps its source venue and instrument.
  2. 02Every value shows when it was received, and its age.
  3. 03Missing data stays missing. It is never turned into zero.
  4. 04Stale data is never presented as live.
  5. 05Derived values say what they were computed from.
  6. 06Coverage limits stay visible.

Method notes
Observations are the last received before the execution time on each venue; their age at that moment is printed, not hidden. Depth may be minutes old.
Coverage and gaps
Venues not collected at the time are listed as not covered. Gaps in collection are stated per venue and per call.
Derived values
Spread = (ask − bid) / mid × 10,000 bps. Depth bands measured against the stated reference price.

Methodology

The method is part of the product.

Two timestamps exist. Age is the difference to now.
Venue timeif supplied · 17:15:52.118
→
Receivedalways · 17:15:52.301
→
Agenow − received · 2 s
There is no stored "written" or "available" time, so none is shown.
01What is collectedTicker, open interest and depth per venue and instrument, each by its own call, each stored with the time it was received.
02How instruments are groupedBy base asset and contract type — BTC perpetual — because no cross-venue canonical ID is published. The selector lists what the store holds.
03Venue time vs received timeVenue time is printed only where the venue sends one. Received time is always present. No other timestamp is stored.
04Age and statusAge is now minus received. LIVE, DELAYED and STALE are computed per call against that call's expected cadence, set by the collector.
05Gaps vs not collected yetA gap is a period inside collection with no observation. Not collected yet is a period before collection began. They look different and are named differently.
06Derived valuesSpread = (ask − bid) / mid × 10,000. OI notional = OI × mark. Depth bands within 10 / 25 / 50 bps of a stated reference. Each shows its inputs.
07When a comparison is validBEST and WORST rank bid, ask, spread and depth among venues whose value is present, live and from an order book, inside each quote-currency group. A figure left out says why in its provenance.
08What the platform cannot knowAnything a venue does not publish, anything between two observations, and anything before collection started.
09Venue-reported vs derivedBid, ask, mark, index, funding and open interest are venue-reported. Spread, notional and depth bands are derived and say so.
