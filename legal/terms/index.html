Terms of use.

Who runs debyko.com, what the service is and is not, and on what terms its data is shown. This page changes only by a dated entry in the list at the end.

Questions about these terms: hello@debyko.com.

debyko.com is operated by MB „Blynai“, a small partnership (mažoji bendrija) registered in the Register of Legal Entities of the Republic of Lithuania, company code 308115000, registered office Girulių g. 10, LT-12112 Vilnius, Lithuania. 02What the service is #

DEBYKO displays crypto market data collected from the public interfaces of trading venues: a live comparison of venues (Arena), historical data (Studio Pro) and an API. The data is collected by the CryptoSmith X market-data engine, operated by the same company.

03What the service is not #

MB „Blynai“ is not a financial institution, an investment manager or a provider of financial services. The company gives no investment advice, does not accept or hold third parties’ money or crypto-assets, and does not operate third parties’ exchange accounts.

debyko.com is not a trading venue: no orders are accepted, executed or routed here. Nothing on the site is an offer, a forecast or individual investment advice.

04Data, as observed #

Data is provided as it was observed, as is and without warranties — in particular without a warranty of completeness. What is collected, on which venue and since when, and the periods in which nothing was observed, are stated on Coverage; gaps are shown as gaps, never filled.

A venue’s own timestamp is printed as the venue sent it. The time the value was received and its age are shown beside it; the rules for every figure are on Methodology. Venues can change, delay or withdraw what they publish at any time. Any decision taken on the basis of the data is the user’s own.

05Subscriptions #

Arena is free. Studio Pro is sold monthly; prices are in the pricing section of the home page. Until a checkout exists, a subscription is requested and arranged by email with hello@debyko.com, and its terms — start, invoice, end — are agreed there before it begins. No payment details are collected on this site.
